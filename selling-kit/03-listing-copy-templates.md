# 03 — Listing copy templates

Fill the `{{ }}` fields from `product-profile.md`. Character limits are exact —
Etsy titles are 140 chars, Redbubble titles are 50.

---

# PART A — ETSY

## A1. Title (140 characters max)

Etsy titles are **keyword strings, not sentences.** Front-load the highest-value
phrase; a buyer only sees roughly the first 40 characters in search results.

**Formula:**
```
[Main search phrase] | [Style/aesthetic] [Product type] | [Subject detail] |
[Recipient or use] | [Occasion or colour]
```

**Worked examples (adapt to your artwork):**

```
Heron Wall Art Printable | Minimalist Bird Print | Wildlife Nature Poster
Instant Download | Birdwatcher Gift | Cottage Decor
```

```
Vintage Botanical Print Set of 6 | Wildflower Wall Art Digital Download |
Cottagecore Nursery Decor | Farmhouse Printable Poster
```

```
Funny Cat Sticker Pack PNG | Kawaii Animal Clipart | Sublimation Design
Bundle | Cricut SVG | Gift for Cat Lovers
```

**Rules:**
- Use all 140 characters. Unused space is wasted ranking surface.
- Separate phrases with `|` or `,` — Etsy reads both fine.
- No ALL CAPS, no "!!!", no "FREE SHIPPING" (that's a separate attribute).
- Never put a brand or character name you don't own in the title.
- Don't repeat the same word more than twice.
- Your top 3 keywords should also appear in your tags and description.

## A2. The 13 tags (20 characters each)

See `05-keywords-and-tags.md` for the full method. Short version: use all 13,
mix broad + specific, include at least 3 multi-word long-tail phrases.

## A3. Description

Etsy descriptions don't strongly affect search ranking, but they decide
**conversion** — and they're legally where AI/production-partner disclosure goes.

```
{{PRODUCT NAME}} — {{one-line description of what it is}}

──────────────────────────────────────
WHAT YOU RECEIVE
──────────────────────────────────────
{{For a digital download:}}
• {{N}} high-resolution JPG files at 300 DPI
• Sizes included: {{list — e.g. 8x10", 11x14", 16x20", 18x24", 24x36", A1-A5}}
• {{Ratio packs / orientations if relevant}}
• Print at home, at a local print shop, or via an online service
• {{Optional bonus: PNG with transparent background / SVG for cutting machines}}

{{For a physical POD product:}}
• Printed on {{blank, e.g. Bella+Canvas 3001, 100% combed cotton}}
• Available in sizes {{XS–3XL}} and {{N}} colours
• Printed and shipped by {{production partner}}
• {{Fit note — e.g. "unisex, runs slightly small, size up for an oversized look"}}

──────────────────────────────────────

──────────────────────────────────────
1. Add to cart and check out.
2. Your files are available instantly — Etsy emails a download link, and you
   can also find them under "Purchases and reviews" in your Etsy account.
3. Download, print, frame, enjoy.

refunds can be given — but if anything is wrong with your files, message me and

──────────────────────────────────────

──────────────────────────────────────

──────────────────────────────────────
{{2–4 sentences. What inspired it, what the style is, where it looks good hung.
```

## A4. Attributes & settings checklist

| Field | What to pick |
|---|---|
| Category | Digital: `Art & Collectibles → Prints → Digital Prints`. Physical: match exactly. |
| Who made it? | "I did" (handmade) / **"A partner or company I work with"** (POD) / see AI note below |
| What is it? | "A finished product" |
| When was it made? | "2020–2026" |
| Creativity attribute | AI-assisted work → **"Designed by a seller"** |
| Production partner | Declare Printify/Printful/Gelato with location + your role |
| Occasion / recipient / style | Fill **every** dropdown. These are free filter facets buyers use. |
| Primary colour + secondary | Fill both. |
| Personalisation | Turn OFF unless you genuinely offer it. |
| Quantity | Digital: 999 (auto-renews). Physical: your real stock. |
| Processing time | Digital: instant. POD: 2–5 business days (match your partner exactly). |
| Shipping profile | POD: use the partner's rates. Self-shipped: measure and weigh first. |
| Returns | Digital: no returns (state it). Physical: 14–30 days recommended; improves conversion. |
| Photos | 10 slots — use all 10. See `04-file-specs-and-upload.md`. |

# PART B — REDBUBBLE

## B1. Title (50 characters max)

Much tighter than Etsy. One clear descriptive phrase — **not** a keyword dump.
Redbubble penalises spammy titles.

```
{{Subject}} — {{Style}} {{Product-word}}
```
Examples:
```
Grey Heron in Reeds — Minimalist Line Art
Vintage Wildflower Botanical Study No. 3
Sleepy Cat Loaf — Kawaii Doodle
```

Count your characters. Anything over 50 gets cut off.

## B2. Description (~500 characters)

```
{{What it is in 1–2 sentences.}} {{Who it's for / where it works — e.g. "A calm,
minimal piece for a bathroom, hallway or birdwatcher's study."}}

{{One line on style/technique: "Drawn as continuous single-weight line art with
a muted sage and slate palette."}}

{{Optional: "Part of my {{Collection Name}} series — see my profile for
matching designs."}}

{{If AI-assisted, tick Redbubble's AI disclosure checkbox and add one honest
line here too.}}
```

## B3. Tags (15 max)

Redbubble's tag field is **comma-separated** and feeds its internal search
directly. Rules:

- Use all 15.
- Order matters — put your strongest first.
- No trademarked terms, no other artists' names, no "redbubble", no "gift for
  him" filler. Tag spam triggers automatic demotion and account strikes.
- Include the literal subject, the style, the colour palette, and 2–3 audience
  terms.

```
{{subject}}, {{subject variant}}, {{style}}, {{aesthetic}}, {{colour}},
{{technique}}, {{theme}}, {{mood}}, {{audience}}, {{hobby}},
{{occasion}}, {{room}}, {{season}}, {{collection name}}, {{medium}}
```

## B4. Product enablement checklist

Redbubble defaults everything ON, and the defaults are usually **wrong**. Go
through every product manually:

- [ ] **Stickers** — set size(s). Enable die-cut, glossy, matte, holographic,
      transparent as appropriate. Bump markup to 50–100%.
- [ ] **T-shirts** — reposition and resize the art on the print area. The default
      placement is almost always too big or too high. Check on light AND dark
      garment colours; if your art has no background, disable dark colours or
      supply an inverted version.
- [ ] **Hoodies / sweatshirts** — same repositioning; smaller than the tee.
- [ ] **Mugs** — wrap placement; check the handle side.
- [ ] **Art prints / posters** — set margins. Redbubble adds a white border by
      default; adjust so it looks intentional.
- [ ] **Phone cases** — critical: the camera cutout will eat part of your art.
      Reposition so nothing important sits top-left.
- [ ] **Tote bags / notebooks / pillows / mouse pads** — resize each.
- [ ] **Duvet covers / blankets / shower curtains** — these need **full-bleed**
      art. A small centred logo looks broken on a duvet. Disable them unless
      your artwork is a seamless pattern or genuinely fills the frame.
- [ ] **Disable any product where the preview looks bad.** A broken preview
      costs you more than the product earns.
- [ ] Check the retail price on each product after setting markup — confirm it's
      sane before publishing.
- [ ] Set markup to **20%** on everything except stickers.

**Save as a collection** so buyers can see related work, and so you can
duplicate settings across future uploads.

# PART C — Both platforms: the 10 listing photos

Etsy gives you 10 slots, Redbubble uses your uploaded artwork plus its own
generated mockups. For Etsy, plan the 10 shots deliberately:

| # | Shot | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero mockup** — the design in context (framed on a wall, on a shirt on a person) | This is your search thumbnail. It decides the click. Must read at 200px wide. |
| 2 | Clean flat artwork on plain background | Shows the actual design clearly |
| 3 | Second mockup, different room/colourway | Broadens appeal |
| 4 | Scale reference — with a person, hand, or furniture | Kills "I thought it was bigger" returns |
| 5 | Detail / close-up crop | Proves print quality and shows craft |
| 6 | What's included — grid of all files/sizes (digital) or all colours (physical) | Answers the #1 pre-purchase question |
| 7 | Lifestyle shot — the buyer's world | Emotional pull |
| 8 | Infographic: sizes / how-it-works / care instructions | Reduces support messages |
| 9 | Alternate colourway or matching items | Upsell |
| 10 | Your brand / shop banner / thank-you card | Builds trust and repeat buyers |

Never leave a slot empty. A 10-photo listing converts materially better than a
3-photo one.
