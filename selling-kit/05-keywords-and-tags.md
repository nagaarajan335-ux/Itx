# 05 — Keywords and tags

Search is the only traffic source that costs nothing and compounds. On Etsy this
is the difference between zero sales and a full-time income.

---

## The core principle

Buyers don't search "art". They search **subject + style + product + recipient +
occasion + colour + room**. Your job is to be present in as many of those
combinations as honestly apply.

A search phrase has three properties:

| | Volume | Competition | Your chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head term** — "wall art" | Huge | Brutal | ~0 as a new shop |
| **Mid term** — "bird wall art" | Good | High | Low |
| **Long-tail** — "minimalist heron line art print bathroom" | Small | Low | **High** |

New shops win on **long-tail**. You cannot out-rank a shop with 40,000 sales on
"wall art". You *can* be the only result for "grey heron line art printable".
Win there, accumulate sales and reviews, then Etsy's algorithm starts trusting
you on broader terms.

---

## Etsy: the 13-tag formula

You get **13 tags, 20 characters each**. Use all 13, always. Untagged listings
are invisible.

Allocate them like this:

| Slots | Type | Example (for a heron illustration) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Exact subject** (what it literally is) | `heron print`, `bird wall art`, `wildlife art` |
| 4–6 | **Style / aesthetic** | `minimalist art`, `line art print`, `botanical style` |
| 7–9 | **Long-tail phrases** (2–3 words, specific) | `heron nursery decor`, `bathroom wall art`, `birdwatcher gift` |
| 10–11 | **Product / format** | `printable wall art`, `digital download` |
| 12–13 | **Occasion / recipient / colour** | `housewarming gift`, `sage green decor` |

**Rules:**
- **20 characters is a hard cap.** `minimalist bird print` = 21 chars → rejected.
  Shorten: `minimal bird print` (18). Count before you type.
- **Don't repeat your title words in all 13 tags.** Etsy already indexes the
  title. Tags should *extend* your reach, not duplicate it. Use them for the
  phrases that didn't fit in the title.
- **Multi-word tags beat single words.** `heron wall art` ranks you for
  "heron", "wall art", and the phrase itself.
- **No keyword stuffing.** Don't tag `disney`, `nike`, or a competitor's shop
  name. That's a policy violation and can close your shop.
- **Include misspellings only if buyers actually search them** (e.g. `colour`
  vs `color` — use whichever your target market uses; Etsy indexes both
  separately).
- **Use singular OR plural, not both** — Etsy stems most of these.

## Etsy: where else keywords live

Etsy indexes these fields, in roughly this order of weight:

1. **Title** — strongest. First 40 chars strongest of all.
2. **Tags** — all 13.
3. **Category and attributes** — the dropdowns. Fill every single one; they're
   filter facets and many buyers shop by filter, not search.
4. **Alt text on images** — small weight, free to add, also an accessibility win.
5. **Description** — minimal search weight, but high conversion weight.
6. **Shop title / About page** — small weight, worth doing once.

Make sure your top 3 phrases appear consistently in title + tags + attributes.
That consistency is what tells the algorithm what you are.

## Redbubble: the 15-tag formula

Comma-separated, up to 15. Redbubble's search is more literal than Etsy's and
penalises spam harder.

**Order matters — strongest first.**

| Slots | Type |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Exact literal subject |
| 4–6 | Style / medium / technique |
| 7–9 | Colour palette and mood |
| 10–12 | Audience / hobby / profession |
| 13–15 | Occasion, room, season, your collection name |

**Hard rules:**
- ❌ No trademarked characters, brands, teams, song titles, celebrity names.
  Redbubble runs an automated IP scanner; a hit removes the design and adds a
  **strike**. Enough strikes = permanent suspension.
- ❌ No other artists' names or shop names.
- ❌ No `redbubble`, `gift`, `gift for him`, `cool`, `awesome`, `best seller`.
  These are tag spam and get you demoted.
- ❌ No tag unrelated to the artwork just because it has volume.
- ✅ Do include your collection/series name — it builds a browsable brand.

## Keyword research method (30 minutes, free)

1. **Etsy's own search bar is the best tool that exists.** Type your subject and
   read the **autocomplete suggestions** — those are literally what buyers type.
   Write down all 10.

2. **Search each suggestion and count results.**
   - Under ~5,000 results = low competition, target it hard.
   - 5,000–50,000 = viable if your listing is strong.
   - Over 200,000 = you will not rank as a new shop. Skip.

3. **Study page 1 of your best term.** For the top 8 listings, note:
   - Their price (this is your price anchor)
   - Their title structure (copy the *pattern*, never the words)
   - Their review count (this tells you how entrenched they are)
   - Their photo #1 style (this tells you what wins the click)

4. **Check the "related searches" and filter facets** Etsy shows — each facet is
   a keyword you should be filling in attributes.

5. **eRank (free tier) or Everbee** — shows search volume and competition
   scores for Etsy terms. Worth an account once you're serious.

6. **For Redbubble**, search your term and look at what's already ranking.
   Redbubble also shows related tags at the bottom of search results — harvest
   those.

7. **Record everything** in a sheet: term, volume/competition, results count,
   top competitor price. Reuse it for every future design in the niche. This
   becomes your most valuable asset.

### Free trademark check (do this before every launch)

| Region | Database |
|---|---|
| USA | USPTO TESS — tmsearch.uspto.gov |
| EU | EUIPO eSearch plus |
| UK | GOV.UK trademark search |
| India | IP India public search |
| Global | WIPO Global Brand Database |

Search your main phrase. If a live trademark exists in **class 16** (printed
matter), **class 25** (clothing), or **class 35** (retail), don't use it in
titles or tags. See `07-legal-ip-tax.md`.

## Iterate — tags are not permanent

This is what separates shops that grow from shops that stall:

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Launch with your best researched 13 tags |
| Day 14 | Check Shop Manager → Stats → **Search terms**. See what actually brought visits. |
| Day 30 | Replace any tag that produced **zero** impressions with a new long-tail variant. |
| Day 60 | Double down: make more listings using the terms that converted. |
| Ongoing | Change **one or two tags at a time**, never all 13. Changing everything resets your indexation and you lose ranking for weeks. |

Etsy's Stats page tells you the exact search phrases buyers used to find you.
That is free, first-party keyword data. Most sellers never look at it.

## Niche angles worth testing

Whatever your artwork is, these framings reliably expand a keyword set beyond
the obvious:

| Angle | Example tags |
|---|---|
| **Profession** | `nurse gift`, `teacher appreciation`, `engineer humor` |
| **Hobby** | `birdwatching gift`, `knitting lover`, `plant mom` |
| **Room** | `bathroom decor`, `nursery wall art`, `office print` |
| **Occasion** | `housewarming`, `wedding gift`, `retirement` |
| **Aesthetic tribe** | `cottagecore`, `dark academia`, `mid century`, `boho`, `japandi` |
| **Life event** | `new baby`, `new home`, `graduation` |
| **Seasonal** | `christmas`, `halloween`, `mothers day` — list 6–8 weeks early |
| **Regional/local** | your state, city, national park, local wildlife |
| **Colour-specific** | `sage green decor`, `terracotta`, `navy and gold` |

One design × 5 of these angles = 5 distinct listings targeting 5 different
audiences. That's how you scale a catalogue without making new art.
