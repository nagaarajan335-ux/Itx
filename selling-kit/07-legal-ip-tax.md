# 07 — Legal, IP, AI disclosure, and tax

Not legal advice. This is the practical risk map. For anything material — real
revenue, a dispute, a takedown notice — talk to a qualified professional in your
jurisdiction.

---

## 1. Intellectual property: the thing that actually ends accounts

Both platforms run automated IP scanners. A hit means: design removed, strike
recorded, repeat strikes = **permanent suspension with your earnings frozen**.
This happens far more often than any other enforcement action.

**Never include, in artwork, titles, tags, or descriptions:**

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Characters | Disney, Marvel, anime, video-game, cartoon characters — including "in the style of" versions |
| Logos & brands | Nike, Apple, sports teams, car marques, fashion houses |
| Celebrity likeness | Actors, musicians, athletes, royalty |
| Lyrics & quotes | Song lyrics, movie lines, book passages, branded slogans |
| Sports | Team names, mascots, league marks — a huge and heavily-policed category |
| Franchise words | "Hogwarts", "Star Wars", "Pokemon" as tags on unrelated art |

**Also prohibited:** reselling someone else's design or a downloaded "AI art
pack" as your own. That's infringement regardless of how the art was made.

### The "fan art" myth

Fan art is **not** protected, tolerated, or safe. It's the single most common
cause of POD account termination. It may sit up for months, then get swept in a
batch takedown — after you've built income you'll lose overnight.

### Trademark search before every launch

| Region | Database |
|---|---|
| USA | USPTO — tmsearch.uspto.gov |
| EU | EUIPO eSearch plus |
| UK | GOV.UK trademark search |
| India | IP India public search |
| Global | WIPO Global Brand Database |

Check your main phrase in **class 16** (printed matter), **class 25** (clothing)
and **class 35** (retail services). A live mark in any of those = don't use the
phrase in titles or tags, even if the artwork itself is original.

### Fonts

Fonts have their own licences. Free-for-personal-use fonts are extremely common
and frequently misused. Check the foundry's EULA for **commercial** and
**products-for-sale** rights — some allow one but not the other. Safe defaults:
Google Fonts (mostly SIL Open Font Licence), Adobe Fonts with a paid plan.

### Stock elements

If your design includes stock imagery, verify the licence covers **print-on-demand
resale**. Many stock licences explicitly exclude "products for resale" or cap
print runs. Extended/commercial licences cost extra and are worth it.

## 2. AI-generated artwork

Policies differ sharply between the two platforms. This is the area people get
wrong most often.

### Etsy — explicit rules, disclosure mandatory

Etsy's **Creativity Standards** address AI directly:

- Seller-prompted AI work belongs in the category **"Designed by a seller"** —
  NOT "Made by a seller", and never represented as handmade.
- **You must disclose AI use in the listing description.** Etsy's own suggested
  wording: *"This artwork was created using AI art generation tools based on my
  original prompts and creative direction."*
- **Production partners must also be disclosed** — if Printify/Printful/Gelato
  prints and ships it, name them and describe your role.
- **Standalone AI prompt bundles are prohibited.** You can sell finished artwork
  made with prompts; you cannot sell the prompts themselves.
- Etsy expects a **genuine creative role** from you: original brief, curation of
  outputs, editing the selected result, accurate representation.

Getting this wrong risks suspension. Getting it right costs you one sentence in
the description.

### Redbubble — permitted, with heavy caveats

- AI art is **allowed**. Redbubble has not issued a blanket ban, and there is no
  special AI prohibition in its content guidelines.
- There is an **AI disclosure checkbox** during upload. This metadata is shown
  publicly to buyers under a "Creation Info" section.
- **New accounts are limited to ~5 AI-tagged uploads per day**; established
  sellers with 100+ sales can do ~20/day.
- Redbubble's User Agreement requires that you hold the **necessary rights** to
  everything you upload. It doesn't grant blanket AI permission so much as treat
  AI output exactly like human output: **you are 100% responsible.**
- ⚠️ **Mass-uploading raw, unedited AI output triggers spam filters.** This can
  lock you into the Standard tier permanently or get the account suspended.
  Curate, edit, and build a coherent style.

### Your AI tool's licence — check this first

Platform permission is only half the story. **Your generator's terms determine
whether you may sell the output at all**, and this varies by *plan*, not just by
tool:

| Tool | Typical position |
|---|---|
| Midjourney | Paid plans grant commercial use; free-trial output historically did not |
| DALL·E / OpenAI | Paid API/ChatGPT usage grants ownership and commercial rights |
| Stable Diffusion | Model-dependent — **check each checkpoint's licence**; some are non-commercial |
| Adobe Firefly | Commercial use permitted for subscribers |
| Free web generators | **Very often non-commercial only.** Read the terms. |

Screenshot or save the licence terms on the day you generate the work. If a tool
changes its terms later, you have evidence of what applied to you.

### Copyright: the uncomfortable truth

The **US Copyright Office** position is that material generated **wholly** by AI
lacks the human authorship required for copyright protection. Human-authored
selection, arrangement, and modification *may* be protected, case by case. A
prompt alone does not make the output yours in a legally enforceable sense.

**What this means practically:** if someone downloads your AI design and
re-uploads it to their own shop, you may lack standing to file an effective DMCA
takedown.

**The fix:** make **substantial human modification** — compositing, significant
repainting, added original typography, vector redraw, colour grading, combining
multiple generations. This does three things at once:
1. Strengthens any copyright claim you might have.
2. Satisfies Etsy's "clear creative role" expectation.
3. Keeps you out of Redbubble's spam filter.

Purely generated, untouched, mass-uploaded AI art is the weakest possible legal
and commercial position. Edited, curated, styled AI-assisted art is defensible.

## 3. Tax

Varies enormously by where you live and sell. The shape of it:

### Marketplace facilitator laws (good news)

In most major markets, **Etsy and Redbubble collect and remit sales tax / VAT /
GST on your behalf** for orders shipped to those jurisdictions:

- **US**: Etsy collects & remits sales tax in all states that impose one on
  marketplace sales.
- **EU / UK**: Etsy collects VAT on orders ≤ €150 / £135.
- **Australia, New Zealand, Norway, Switzerland, Canada, Mexico, India**: similar
  marketplace collection regimes.

This removes the worst of the compliance burden. It does **not** remove your
income tax obligations.

### What remains yours

| Obligation | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Income tax on profit** | Everywhere. You are running a business. Fees and COGS are deductible expenses — keep receipts. |
| **Self-employment tax** | US: Schedule C + Schedule SE, ~15.3% on net earnings over $400. UK: Class 2/4 NI. India: business income + possible GST registration. |
| **VAT/GST registration thresholds** | UK: £90k turnover. EU: varies by state. India: ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh special states) — and **export of services** rules may still require registration. Malaysia: RM500k for SST. Australia: A$75k. |
| **Etsy's regulatory operating fee** | 0.25%–1.1% in ~10 countries including UK, France, Italy, Spain, Turkey. Check whether it applies to you. |
| **Redbubble's tax forms** | Redbubble/Artbox may ask for a W-8BEN (non-US) or W-9 (US) for royalty reporting. |
| **Import duties for buyers** | Usually the buyer's problem, but state it in your policies to avoid disputes. |

### Practical setup

- [ ] Track **every** fee, subscription, sample, mockup purchase, and software
      cost — all deductible.
- [ ] Keep a separate bank account for the shop from day one. Cheap insurance
      against a miserable tax season.
- [ ] Set aside **25–30% of profit** for tax until you know your real rate.
- [ ] Record the date and amount of each platform payout.
- [ ] If you're in India or Malaysia and selling to US/EU customers, the
      **export-of-services** treatment is usually favourable but has paperwork
      (LUT / GST registration). Ask a local accountant — one consultation is
      worth many times its cost.
- [ ] Etsy issues **1099-K** (US) / equivalent local forms when you cross
      reporting thresholds. Redbubble issues royalty statements.

## 4. Platform policies worth knowing

| Rule | Platform | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Don't review your own listing or ask friends to | Etsy | Suspension |
| Don't incentivise reviews ("5 stars for a discount") | Both | Listing removal / suspension |
| Don't run two shops selling the same designs | Etsy | Both closed |
| Don't keyword-stuff tags with unrelated terms | Both | Demotion / suspension |
| Don't list a design you can't actually fulfil | Both | Refunds + account damage |
| Don't circumvent Etsy to take payment off-platform | Etsy | Permanent ban |
| Do declare production partners | Etsy | Required; non-disclosure is a violation |
| Do respond to messages within 24h | Both | Etsy tracks response rate; it affects search ranking |
| Do ship within your stated processing time | Both | Late dispatch destroys your ranking and reviews |

## 5. Quick risk self-audit

Answer honestly. Any "no" here should be fixed **before** you launch.

- [ ] Every element in my artwork is mine, licensed, or public domain.
- [ ] No characters, logos, brands, teams, celebrities, lyrics or quotes appear.
- [ ] My fonts are licensed for products-for-sale.
- [ ] Any stock used permits POD resale.
- [ ] If AI-assisted: my tool's **paid plan** grants commercial rights, and I've
      saved the terms.
- [ ] If AI-assisted: I've made substantial human edits and can describe them.
- [ ] I've trademark-searched my main phrases in classes 16/25/35.
- [ ] I've disclosed AI use in the description and selected "Designed by a seller".
- [ ] I've declared my production partner.
- [ ] I've read the current User Agreement on both platforms (they change).
- [ ] I have a separate bank account and an expense record.
- [ ] I've ordered and inspected a physical sample.
