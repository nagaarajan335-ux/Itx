# 06 — 30-day launch plan

A realistic day-by-day. Most of this is unglamorous; it's also what actually
works.

---

## Before you start: account setup

### Etsy
- [ ] Create the shop (you need a Google/email account and a bank account).
- [ ] Pay the **$15–$29 one-time shop setup fee**.
- [ ] Complete **identity verification** (government ID + bank details). Shops
      that stall here never open. Do it first.
- [ ] Set shop currency = **your bank account's currency**. This avoids the 2.5%
      conversion fee on every payout.
- [ ] Shop name: memorable, spellable, not trademarked, `.com` domain ideally
      available too.
- [ ] Shop icon (500×500) and banner (3,360×840).
- [ ] About page: a real photo of you and 3–4 sentences. Shops with a filled
      About page convert better — buyers want to know a human is behind it.
- [ ] Policies: returns, exchanges, shipping times. Copy Etsy's defaults and
      customise.
- [ ] Settings → **Offsite Ads**: leave ON initially (you need the traffic).
- [ ] Declare any **production partner** (Printify/Printful/Gelato).

### Redbubble
- [ ] Sign up (free).
- [ ] Complete profile: avatar, cover image, bio, links.
- [ ] Check **Artist Dashboard → Account Tiers and Fees** to confirm your tier
      and read the exact fee you'll pay.
- [ ] Add payout details. Note the **$20 minimum** — earnings roll over until
      you hit it.
- [ ] Read the User Agreement, IP/Publicity Rights Policy, and Community &
      Content Guidelines once. Genuinely. Most suspensions are avoidable.

### Both
- [ ] Dedicated email address for the shop.
- [ ] A simple spreadsheet: date, platform, product, price, COGS, fees, profit,
      status. You will lose money without this.

## Week 1 — Prepare (before listing anything)

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect your artwork. Confirm resolution and transparency (`04`). Decide digital vs physical vs both. |
| 1 | Fill in `product-profile.md` completely. |
| 2 | Keyword research: 30 minutes using `05`. Build your term list and check results counts. |
| 2 | Trademark-search your top 5 phrases. |
| 3 | Build the **master file**: 5,000 × 5,500 px, 300 DPI, sRGB, transparent PNG. |
| 3 | Export all derivatives: Etsy photos (10 × 2,000 px JPG @ 80%), digital ratio ZIPs (<20 MB each), POD-ready 4,500 × 5,400. |
| 4 | Make mockups. Printify/Printful generators are free. For digital art, use free frame mockups or buy a $5 pack — good mockups are the highest-ROI spend in this entire plan. |
| 5 | Write the title (140 chars), 13 tags, and description from `03`. |
| 5 | Run the price through `profit-calculator.py`. Record the answer in `product-profile.md` §7. |
| 6 | **Order samples.** One sticker, one shirt, one print. Non-negotiable — you must see and touch what you're selling. |
| 7 | Upload to **Redbubble** first (it's faster and lower stakes). Enable products one by one, reposition art, set markup 20% (stickers 50–100%). Save as a collection. |

---

## Week 2 — Launch on Etsy

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 8 | Publish your first Etsy listing with all 10 photos and every attribute filled. |
| 8 | **Buy your own listing** (or have a friend do it) to test the digital download delivery end-to-end. Nothing is worse than discovering broken files after a real customer does. *Note: don't review your own listing — that violates policy.* |
| 9 | Create **variants**: 3–5 colourway versions of the same design, each its own listing. Same work, 5× the search surface. |
| 10 | Create a **bundle listing** (all variants together at a higher price). Bundles earn more per sale. |
| 11 | Add 2–3 more designs. Volume matters more than perfection. |
| 12 | Cross-link every listing in its description ("More from my shop"). |
| 13 | Write 5 Pinterest pins per listing. Pinterest is Etsy's largest external traffic source and pins compound for months. |
| 14 | Review Shop Manager → Stats. Confirm impressions are registering. |

**Target by end of week 2:** 8–15 live Etsy listings, 1 Redbubble work with all
products configured, samples ordered.

## Week 3 — Distribute

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 15 | Upload the same master file to **TeePublic** (15 tags, flat royalties, zero platform fees — better per-unit than Redbubble Standard). |
| 16 | Upload to **Society6** if your work suits wall art / home decor. |
| 17 | Set up **Pinterest business account**. Create a board per collection. Pin every listing with a keyword-rich description. |
| 18 | **Instagram / TikTok**: post the process, not just the product. A 15-second screen recording of you making the design routinely outperforms a polished product shot. |
| 19 | Post to relevant Reddit / Facebook / Discord communities **where self-promotion is allowed**. Read each community's rules first. Most ban direct selling; most allow "I made this" in designated threads. |
| 20 | Apply to **Amazon Merch on Demand** (approval takes weeks — apply early even if you don't use it yet). |
| 21 | Add 2 more designs. |

---

## Week 4 — Measure and iterate

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 22 | Read Etsy **Stats → Search terms**. Which phrases brought visits? |
| 22 | Identify listings with **impressions but no clicks** → the problem is photo #1. Remake the hero mockup. |
| 23 | Identify listings with **clicks but no sales** → the problem is price, description, or reviews. Adjust one variable. |
| 23 | Replace any tag with **zero impressions** after 2+ weeks. Change only 1–2 tags per listing. |
| 24 | Check samples. Does print quality match what you promised? Fix the artwork or drop the product. |
| 25 | Ask your first buyers for a review (politely, in the delivery note or a follow-up message — never incentivised, that's a policy violation). |
| 26 | Add 2 more designs in your best-performing niche. |
| 27 | Consider **Etsy Ads at $1/day** on your single best listing only. Do not run ads on listings that don't convert organically — you'll just pay to prove they don't work. |
| 28 | Reconcile the month: revenue, fees, COGS, profit. Update your spreadsheet. |
| 29 | Write down the 3 things that worked and the 3 that didn't. |
| 30 | Plan month 2: **20 more listings** in the niches that showed any signal. |

## The growth loop

```
   More listings  ──→  More search surface  ──→  More impressions
        ▲                                              │
        │                                              ▼
   Learn what                                     More visits
   worked  ←──  Measure  ←──  Sales + reviews  ←──  Better CTR
```

The loop only turns if you keep feeding it listings. **Volume is the strongest
predictor of income on both platforms** — not the brilliance of any one design.

One good design → 5 colourways → 3 product formats → 2 seasonal variants =
**30 listings**. Twenty designs done that way = 600 listings. That's a shop with
real search presence.

## Marketing channels, ranked by effort-to-payoff

| Channel | Effort | Payoff | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Etsy SEO** | Medium | ★★★★★ | The only compounding free channel. Do this first, always. |
| **Pinterest** | Low–Med | ★★★★★ | Pins live for months/years. Best external source for Etsy. Use keyword-rich pin descriptions, not hashtags. |
| **Listing volume** | High | ★★★★★ | Boring, unbeatable. |
| **Bundles & upsells** | Low | ★★★★ | Raises average order value instantly. |
| **TikTok / Reels** | Medium | ★★★★ | Process videos and "pack an order with me" perform well. One viral video can change a shop. |
| **Instagram** | Medium | ★★★ | Slow now, but builds brand and repeat buyers. |
| **Etsy Ads** | Low | ★★★ | Only after organic conversion is proven. Start $1/day. |
| **Email list** | Medium | ★★★★ | Include a card/link in digital delivery files. Repeat sales are pure profit. |
| **Reddit / FB groups** | Low | ★★ | Only where self-promo is allowed. Easily backfires. |
| **Influencer seeding** | Medium | ★★ | Send free product to micro-accounts in your niche. |
| **Paid social ads** | High | ★ | Don't. Not until you're doing $500+/month organically. |

## What "good" looks like

Be honest with yourself against these, monthly:

| Metric | Month 1 | Month 3 | Month 6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live listings | 10–20 | 50–80 | 150+ |
| Etsy visits/day | 0–5 | 10–40 | 50–200 |
| Conversion rate | — | 1–2% | 2–3% |
| Reviews | 0 | 5–15 | 40+ |
| Monthly profit | ~$0 | $20–150 | $100–500 |

If you're well below these at month 3, the cause is almost always one of:
1. **Not enough listings** (by far the most common)
2. Weak hero photo (impressions but no clicks)
3. Wrong niche / saturated keyword (impressions near zero)
4. Price too high for the perceived value (clicks but no sales)

Diagnose from the data, change one variable, wait two weeks, measure again.
