## Recommended starting prices

Every figure below is generated by `profit-calculator.py`. Etsy numbers are for
a **US seller** (3% + $0.25 processing) — adjust with `--country` if you're
elsewhere. Redbubble figures assume **Standard tier**, which is where every new
account starts.

### Etsy — digital downloads (zero COGS, no shipping)

| Product | Price | Etsy fees | Effective rate | **Your profit** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single print | $4.99 | $0.92 | 18.5% | **$4.07** |
| Single print | $7.99 | $1.21 | 15.1% | **$6.78** |
| Bundle of 5–10 prints | $12.99 | $1.68 | 13.0% | **$11.31** |
| Bundle of 5–10 prints | $19.99 | $2.35 | 11.8% | **$17.64** |
| SVG / cut-file bundle | $3.99 | $0.83 | 20.8% | **$3.16** |
| SVG / cut-file bundle | $8.99 | $1.30 | 14.5% | **$7.69** |

Bundles are the play: a $19.99 bundle nets **$17.64**, more than four separate
$4.99 singles ($16.28) for a fraction of the listing-fee and SEO overhead.

### Etsy — physical via POD (buyer pays $5 shipping on top)

| Product | Price | COGS (production + ship to buyer) | Etsy fees | **Your profit** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-shirt | $24.99 | $13.50 | $3.30 | **$13.19** |
| T-shirt | $28.99 | $13.50 | $3.68 | **$16.81** |
| Hoodie | $44.99 | $28.00 | $5.20 | **$16.79** |
| Hoodie | $54.99 | $30.00 | $6.15 | **$23.84** |
| Mug 11oz | $16.99 | $8.50 | $2.54 | **$10.95** |
| Mug 11oz | $19.99 | $9.50 | $2.82 | **$12.67** |

COGS is the estimate to beat — get real quotes from Printify, Printful and
Gelato for your specific blank, because a $2 difference in COGS is a $2
difference in profit on every single sale.

### Etsy — self-shipped / handmade

| Product | Price | Realistic profit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sticker | $3.50 – $5.00 | $0.90 – $2.30 | After ~$0.40 print + ~$1.20 postage + materials. Volume business. |
| Physical art print | $18.00 – $45.00 | $10 – $30 | Depends on size, paper stock, rigid-mailer postage. |
| Framed print via POD | $45.00 – $85.00 | $15 – $30 | Highest order value; slower to sell. |

| Throw pillow | $20.00 | $24.00 | **$2.00** | $3.20 |
| Mouse pad | $15.00 | $18.00 | **$1.50** | $2.40 |

**Stickers are the deliberate exception to the 20% rule.** Base price is tiny
and buyers are price-insensitive on a $3 item, so push markup higher:

| Sticker markup | Retail | You get (Standard) | You get (Premium) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50% | $2.98 | **$0.35** | $0.56 |
| 100% | $3.98 | **$0.60** | $0.96 |

Tripling your margin percentage (50% → 100%) earns you $0.25 more per sticker.
Worth it, because a $3.98 sticker still converts — but understand that stickers
are a traffic-and-review builder, not an income stream. To clear Redbubble's
**$20 payout threshold** on Standard tier, stickers alone would take:

| Markup | Net per sticker | Sales needed to reach $20 |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | $0.20 | **101** |
| 50% | $0.35 | **57** |
| 100% | $0.60 | **34** |

Under $20, your earnings simply roll over to the next month — you're never out
of pocket, but you also don't get paid. Most new artists sit below the threshold
for their first few months.

On everything else, stay at **20%**. The excess markup fee eats most of what you
gain above it, and a higher retail price costs you conversion.

⚠️ **Base prices are approximate and vary by product size and by the buyer's
country.** Redbubble revises them periodically. Read the live figure from the
"Edit products" screen when you upload, then re-run:

```bash
python3 profit-calculator.py redbubble --base <REAL_BASE> --markup 20 --tier standard
```

## Worked examples

### A. $6.00 digital download on Etsy (US seller)

```
Item price                              $6.00
Listing fee                             −$0.20
Transaction fee (6.5%)                  −$0.39
Payment processing (3% + $0.25)         −$0.43
                                        ──────
Total fees                              −$1.02   (17.0%)
PROFIT                                  +$4.98   (83.0% margin)
```

No COGS, no shipping, no inventory. This is why digital downloads are the best
starting point.

### B. Same design, Redbubble Standard tier, 20% markup

| Product | Retail | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Sticker (base $1.99) | $2.39 | $0.20 |
| Classic tee (base $20) | $24.00 | $2.00 |
| Hoodie (base $40) | $48.00 | $4.00 |
| Art print (base $19) | $22.80 | $1.90 |

Best case across the whole catalogue: **$4.00**. Compare with **$4.98 for one
digital download on Etsy.**

### C. $25 tee on Etsy via Printify, +$5 shipping, $12 COGS

```
Item price                              $25.00
Shipping charged                         $5.00
Order total                             $30.00
Listing fee                             −$0.20
Transaction fee (6.5% of $30)           −$1.95
Payment processing (3% + $0.25)         −$1.15
                                        ──────
Etsy fees                               −$3.30   (11.0%)
Production cost (Printify)              −$12.00
                                        ──────
PROFIT                                  +$14.70  (49.0% margin)
```

**$14.70 on Etsy vs $2.00 on Redbubble for the same shirt.** That 7× gap is the
entire argument for doing both but prioritising Etsy.

### D. What an Offsite Ads sale does to you

```
$30 item + $6 shipping, UK seller, 0.4% regulatory fee, Offsite Ad attributed

Listing fee                             −$0.20
Transaction fee (6.5%)                  −$2.34
Payment processing (4% + £0.20)         −$1.64
Regulatory operating fee (0.4%)         −$0.14
Offsite Ads (15%)                       −$5.40
                                        ──────
Total fees                              −$9.72   (27.0% of order)
```

Under $10k/year in sales you can **opt out** of Offsite Ads (Shop Manager →
Settings → Offsite Ads). Over $10k it becomes mandatory at 12%. New sellers:
leave it ON at first — you need the traffic more than you need the 15%, and it
only charges on sales it actually drove.

## The three pricing rules that matter most

### 1. Never price under $4 on Etsy

Flat fees ($0.20 + $0.25) dominate cheap listings.

| Price | Fees | Effective rate | Profit (no COGS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2.99 | $0.73 | **24.6%** | $2.26 |
| $4.99 | $0.92 | 18.5% | $4.07 |
| $9.99 | $1.40 | 14.0% | $8.59 |
| $14.99 | $1.87 | 12.5% | $13.12 |
| $24.99 | $2.82 | 11.3% | $22.17 |

A $2.99 listing surrenders a quarter of itself. If your item is genuinely worth
$2.99, **bundle five of them at $9.99** — same work, better margin, higher
average order value.

### 2. Price on profit, not on the competition

Work backwards from a target:

```bash
python3 profit-calculator.py etsy --price 20 --shipping 5 --cogs 12 \
        --solve --target-profit 8
# → price the item at $17.60 (round to $17.99)
```

Then check that price against 10 competitors. If you're wildly above them, your
COGS is wrong (change POD provider) or your listing needs to justify the premium
(better mockups, bundling, framing). Do **not** just cut the price until you
match the cheapest seller — they're often losing money.

Use charm pricing: `.99`, `.95`, `.00`. Round up to the nearest one, never down.

### 3. Your margin target

| Business type | Minimum margin after all fees & COGS |
|---|---|
| Digital download | **70%+** (should be 80–85%) |
| POD physical | **35%+** |
| Self-shipped physical | **50%+** (you're paying for labour and postage) |

Below those lines you don't have a business, you have a hobby that costs you
money. The calculator flags it:

```
!! WARNING: you LOSE money on this listing.
! Thin margin (<30%). Consider raising price or cutting COGS.
```

## Costs people forget

| Cost | Reality |
|---|---|
| Etsy listing renewals | $0.20 every 4 months **per active listing**, whether it sells or not. 100 listings = $60/year. |
| Etsy shop setup fee | $15–$29 one-time, new shops. |
| Multi-quantity fee | $0.20 per extra unit when one order buys several. |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% if listing currency ≠ payment account currency. Set your shop currency to your bank's currency to avoid this entirely. |
| Sales tax / VAT | Etsy collects & remits in most jurisdictions — but check your own obligations (`07-legal-ip-tax.md`). |
| POD samples | Order 1–2 samples of every product type. $30–60, non-negotiable. You must verify print quality before selling it. |
| Mockups | Free (Printify/Printful generators) or $5–15 for good ones on Etsy/Creative Market. |
| Your time | Customer service, review replies, social posts. Count it. |
| Etsy Ads | Optional, min $1/day. Don't run ads until your listing converts organically. |

## Realistic income expectations

Being straight with you, because most guides aren't:

| Month | Typical result for a new shop with 10–30 listings |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | $0 – $20. You're building indexation and reviews, not income. |
| 3–6 | $20 – $150/mo if your SEO is decent and your niche isn't saturated. |
| 6–12 | $100 – $500/mo with 100+ listings and a repeatable niche. |
| 12+ | $500+/mo requires 200+ listings, real branding, or an external audience. |

Redbubble alone rarely exceeds $50/month at Standard tier unless you have
hundreds of designs. **Volume of listings is the single strongest predictor of
income on both platforms** — not the quality of any one design.

The winning play: one good design → 10 product variants → 5 colourway versions →
3 seasonal versions → 25 listings. Multiply that by 20 designs and you have a
shop that earns.
